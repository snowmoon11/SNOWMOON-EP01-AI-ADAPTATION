# 🌙 SNOWMOON — AI Visual Adaptation

**An independent AI filmmaking project inspired by Snowmoon by Vitalik Buterin.**

We are a community of Snowmoon fans exploring how AI can bring the novel's characters, ideas, and imagined universe to life.

## 🎬 Episode 01 — DZEGO WILL RISE AGAIN

Our first episode is an approximately 5-minute AI science-fiction short film told across eight scenes.

It explores knowledge, friendship, decentralized cooperation, and the resilience of Dzego.

## 📂 Open Production Materials

This repository includes:

- English screenplay and scene structure
- AI image-generation prompts
- AI video-generation prompts
- Character and visual continuity references
- Production workflow notes
