# SNOWMOON
## EPISODE 01 — DZEGO WILL RISE AGAIN

**Adapted from Chapter Two of *Snowmoon* by Vitalik Buterin**

**Runtime:** Approximately 5 minutes 15 seconds  
**Structure:** 8 scenes  
**Format:** AI-assisted science fiction short film

---

## SCENE 01 — UNDERGROUND UNIVERSITY: COLD OPEN

**Timecode:** 00:00–00:25

**VISUAL**

A vast underground university lecture hall. Tiered seating accommodates dozens of students. A massive projection screen dominates the front of the room.

The walls are covered with silver anti-wireless-signal material, decorated with colorful educational illustrations of cartoon animals and scientific equipment.

Zei and Fin sit beside each other. Zei is thinking about the instructor's question while Fin watches him.

At the front, the instructor stands beside two transparent containers displaying gas molecules at different temperatures.

**INSTRUCTOR:**
"Zei, what happens if we could reverse the process perfectly?"

**ZEI:**
"Then we could recover information that seemed to be lost. And if we repeated it, we could compress more and more information into less space."

**INSTRUCTOR:**
"Exactly. But—"

Suddenly, a deafening explosion comes from a nearby room.

The classroom shakes violently. The camera loses stability. Part of the wall and floor collapses, sending dust and debris across the room.

Zei is knocked down by the impact. A piece of falling debris strikes his left arm.

Fin reaches out to grab him.

**CUT TO BLACK.**

**TITLE CARD:**

A FEW MINUTES EARLIER

## SCENE 02 — DZEGO: HUN MIN COMMERCIAL STREET

**Timecode:** 00:25–01:10

**VISUAL**

A massive underground urban and industrial district.

Public transportation arrives at a station. Large numbers of pedestrians move through busy streets lined with one- and two-story buildings and colorful electronic signs.

Shops display handheld devices, watches, computing equipment, sensors, and batteries.

Small workshops manufacture precision electronic products.

Zei and Fin step off public transportation and walk side by side along the street.

**FIN:**
"How was your Minpentai game last night?"

**ZEI:**
"Third place. I finally found a wall pattern that sent their gliders right back."

**FIN:**
"I spent the whole evening studying geography."

**ZEI:**
"Still memorizing all those voting percentages?"

**FIN:**
"Unfortunately. I would rather be playing Minpentai."

They laugh and continue walking.

A friendly security drone flies overhead. Their wrist devices briefly light up, completing an identity and security verification.

Neither of them pays much attention to the process.

## SCENE 03 — THE SECRET CLASSROOM LOCATION

**Timecode:** 01:10–01:55

**VISUAL**

Zei checks his wrist device.

The screen displays a newly decrypted classroom location and time, accompanied by a yellow verification symbol.

**ZEI:**
"The classroom location is out."

**FIN:**
"Again? Have they changed the security system?"

**ZEI:**
"Looks like it. The Arctic Empire is getting more aggressive."

The two quickly check their route and turn into an underground passage.

A young woman standing ahead calls out to them.

**YOUNG WOMAN:**
"Fin! Over here!"

She leads them down a staircase.

When they reach the originally assigned location, a staff member stops them.

**STAFF MEMBER:**
"Change of plans. Opposite room. One floor up."

Fin looks at Zei.

**FIN:**
"Again?"

They return to the floor above and enter another spacious classroom.

---

## SCENE 04 — UNDERGROUND UNIVERSITY: PHYSICS CLASS

**Timecode:** 01:55–03:05

**VISUAL**

The classroom has silver anti-wireless-signal material covering its walls.

Educational murals display cartoon animals, calculators, chemistry equipment, and green trees.

At the front of the room, a cartoon pig holding a calculator and a cartoon hamster holding a bottle of green liquid stand beside the university motto:

"One man learning will set one man free. A nation learning will make the nation great."

The instructor enters.

**INSTRUCTOR:**
"Welcome to physics class. Today, we're going to talk about something called entropy."

The students gradually become quiet.

The instructor activates his wrist device. Two transparent containers appear on the large display.

**INSTRUCTOR:**
"Imagine two containers. One has hot gas, the other has cold gas. When they mix, the heat spreads out. But what happens to the information about every single molecule?"

Some students raise their hands. Others discuss the question with their classmates.

Zei raises his hand.

**INSTRUCTOR:**
"Yes, Zei?"

**ZEI:**
"If we could reverse the mixing perfectly, we could get all those details back. Then we could repeat the process and compress more information into less space. That would be impossible."

The instructor nods thoughtfully.

**INSTRUCTOR:**
"Excellent. The information doesn't simply disappear. The uncertainty moves into details we may not need to know."

The instructor looks at Zei.

**INSTRUCTOR:**
"That was a remarkable answer. What's your name?"

**ZEI:**
"Zei."

**INSTRUCTOR:**
"Zei, you have understood—"

---

## SCENE 05 — THE EXPLOSION: THE VISUAL CLIMAX

**Timecode:** 03:05–03:45

**VISUAL**

A massive explosive sound suddenly comes from a nearby room.

In an instant, the entire underground classroom shakes violently.

The camera cuts from a close-up of the instructor to an ultra-wide view of the lecture hall.

The walls shake. Part of the floor collapses. Pieces of the wall and clouds of dust spread throughout the classroom.

Students quickly stand up. Desks and chairs are thrown out of position.

Zei is struck on his left arm by falling debris and falls to the ground.

Fin immediately turns around, searching for Zei.

The instructor calls out loudly.

**INSTRUCTOR:**
"Is everyone okay? Anyone injured?"

The camera moves through the chaos and settles on Zei.

Fin grabs him and helps him up.

**FIN:**
"Zei! Are you alright?"

**ZEI:**
"My arm... I can't move it properly."
